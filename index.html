Professional Profile

Evans Otieno Ombato is an ICT professional whose work sits at the intersection of systems administration and academic instruction. He combines hands-on expertise in network infrastructure, database administration and health-information systems with proven experience designing CDACC-aligned curricula and delivering competency-based ICT instruction in TVET institutions.
